Shaped small beads wait here, off the main line, until there is enough work
in one area for one builder session and one review. No agent works this
stage.

A bead here is shaped and ready; it waits for company, not for shaping.
When the yardmaster bundles the siding's beads, either they are folded into
one bead or they go as a train whose wagons are these beads.

The stage has no outcomes; the yardmaster (or mckean) moves the bead
(`yardr bead advance <id> --to <stage>`):

- to `new`, when it is the bundle's carrier or goes alone;
- to `backlog`, when it is folded into another bead (then closed as
  `folded into <id>`) or needs reshaping.
